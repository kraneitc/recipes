## Ingredients

### Dry mixture

*1 large bowl (about 3 L).*

- 1 cup (90 g) rolled oats
- 1 cup (150 g) plain flour
- 1 cup (220 g) brown sugar
- 3/4 cup (60 g) desiccated coconut

### Butter and golden syrup (add directly to a small saucepan)

- 125 g butter
- 2 tbsp golden syrup

### Bicarbonate mixture and dough

*1 small heatproof cup (about 250 mL).*

- 1 tsp bicarbonate of soda (baking soda)
- 2 tbsp boiling water

## Ingredient Shopping

### Baking and pantry

- 1 cup (90 g) rolled oats
- 1 cup (150 g) plain flour
- 1 cup (220 g) brown sugar
- 3/4 cup (60 g) desiccated coconut
- 2 tbsp golden syrup
- 1 tsp bicarbonate of soda (baking soda)

### Dairy and chilled

- 125 g butter

## Method

### Dry mixture

1. Preheat the oven to 160°C fan-forced (180°C conventional). Line two baking trays with baking paper.
2. Combine the prepared rolled oats, flour, brown sugar, and coconut in the large bowl.

### Butter and golden syrup (add directly to a small saucepan)

3. Put the prepared butter and golden syrup in a small saucepan. Melt together over low heat, stirring until smooth, then remove from the heat.

### Bicarbonate mixture and dough

4. Stir the prepared bicarbonate of soda into the boiling water in the heatproof cup; the mixture will foam.
5. Carefully stir the bicarbonate mixture into the melted butter and golden syrup.
6. Pour the wet mixture into the prepared dry mixture and stir until no dry patches remain.
7. Roll tablespoon-sized portions of dough into balls and arrange them on the lined trays, leaving room for them to spread. Flatten each ball slightly with your hand or the back of a spoon.
8. Bake for 10–12 minutes for chewy biscuits or 13–15 minutes for crisp biscuits. The biscuits should be golden, with slightly darker edges; they will firm as they cool.
9. Cool on the trays for 5 minutes, then transfer to a wire rack to cool completely.

## Notes

- Store the cooled biscuits in an airtight container for up to 1 week.
- The balance of oats, coconut, brown sugar, butter, and golden syrup gives these biscuits their characteristic rich flavour and texture.
- The 15-minute preparation time is an estimate; the supplied recipe did not specify a preparation time.

## Variations

- For extra chewiness, increasing the golden syrup from 2 tbsp to as much as 3 tbsp is an untested variation from the supplied notes.
- For crunchier biscuits, flatten them more before baking and bake them for an extra 1–2 minutes, watching the edges closely. This is an untested variation from the supplied notes.
