## Ingredients

### Aromatic base

*1 small jug (about 250 mL) for the oil; 1 large bowl (about 2 L) for the onion; 2 small bowls (about 500 mL each), one for the apple and one for the garlic and ginger.*

- 4 tbsp neutral oil
- 2 large onions, finely chopped
- 1 small apple, peeled and grated
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 tbsp grated fresh ginger, or 2 tsp ground ginger

### Spice and tomato base

*1 small bowl (about 250 mL) for the spices; 1 small bowl (about 125 mL) for the tomato paste, kept separate.*

- 4 tbsp mild or medium curry powder
- 2 tsp ground cumin
- 2 tsp ground coriander
- 2 tbsp tomato paste, keep separate

### Chicken

*1 large bowl (about 3 L) for the chicken; 1 small ramekin for the salt, kept separate.*

- 1 kg chicken thighs, cut into large chunks
- Salt, to taste, keep separate

### Sauce and thickening

*1 large jug (about 2 L) for the coconut milk, coconut cream, and stock; 1 small bowl (about 500 mL) for the chutney, sultanas, and almond meal.*

- 1 x 400 mL can full-fat coconut milk
- 1 x 400 mL can coconut cream
- 1 cup chicken stock or water
- 3 tbsp mango chutney
- 4 tbsp sultanas
- 3 tbsp almond meal (ground almonds)

### Vegetables

*1 large bowl (about 2 L), kept frozen until needed.*

- 4 cups frozen mixed vegetables

### To finish

*1 small bowl (about 250 mL) for the butter; 2 small ramekins, one for the extra chutney and one for the optional chilli, kept separate.*

- 1–2 tbsp butter
- Up to 2 tbsp mango chutney, to taste, keep separate
- Chilli flakes or finely chopped fresh chilli, to taste (optional), keep separate

## Method

### Aromatic base

1. Heat the prepared oil in a large, heavy pot over medium heat. Add the onion and cook for 10–12 minutes, stirring occasionally, until soft and lightly golden.
2. Add the grated apple and cook for 2 minutes to soften.
3. Add the garlic and ginger and cook, stirring, for 30 seconds.

### Spice and tomato base

4. Add the prepared spices and the tomato paste. Cook for 1–2 minutes, stirring constantly, until fragrant and slightly darkened. This deepens the colour and cooks out the raw tomato flavour.

### Chicken

5. Add the chicken and season with some of the prepared salt. Stir until every piece is coated and lightly coloured; the chicken does not need to cook through at this stage.

### Sauce and thickening

6. Pour in the prepared coconut milk, coconut cream, and stock mixture. Add the prepared chutney, sultanas, and almond meal, stir well, and bring to a gentle simmer.
7. Reduce the heat and simmer uncovered for 35–45 minutes, stirring occasionally. Keep the sauce gently bubbling rather than at a rolling boil. It is ready for the vegetables when it coats the back of a spoon and oil begins to separate around the edges.

### Vegetables

8. Stir in the frozen mixed vegetables. Simmer for 8–10 minutes, until the vegetables are tender and heated through and the chicken is cooked through.

### To finish

9. Turn off the heat and stir in 1 tbsp of the prepared butter. Add the remaining butter for extra richness, if desired.
10. Taste and adjust with the prepared salt, optional chilli, and up to 2 tbsp extra chutney for more sweetness.

## Notes

- When hot, the sauce should be thick but spoonable, leave a clear trail when a spoon is dragged through it, and not pool watery liquid around rice.
- The curry freezes well. Some separation after thawing is normal; reheat it gently, then simmer and stir until the sauce comes back together.
- The flavour improves significantly overnight.

## Variations

- Using water in place of chicken stock is an untested option from the source notes.
- Ground ginger in place of fresh ginger is an untested option from the source notes.
