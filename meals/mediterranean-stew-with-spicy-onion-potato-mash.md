# Mediterranean Stew with Spicy Onion & Potato Mash Batch

A batch-cooked meal of hearty lentil, vegetable, and quinoa stew with warm, spiced onion and potato mash, portioned together for later serving.

## Components

- [Mediterranean Lentil, Vegetable & Quinoa Stew](../recipes/mediterranean-lentil-vegetable-quinoa-stew/recipe.md) — 1 full recipe (8 servings)
- [Spicy Onion & Potato Mash](../recipes/spicy-onion-potato-mash/recipe.md) — 1 full recipe (10–12 component servings)

## Coordination

- **T−100 minutes:** Prepare the ingredients for both recipes, keeping each recipe's activity groups separate.
- **T−80 minutes:** Begin the stew's vegetable base and continue through the lentil simmer.
- **T−50 minutes:** Put the mash potatoes on to cook while the stew simmers. Cook the mash's onion and spices while the potatoes boil.
- **T−40 minutes:** Add the quinoa and remaining vegetables to the stew.
- **T−20 minutes:** Add the greens, lemon, and pepper to the stew. Turn off the heat and let the stew stand for 10–15 minutes while you drain, mash, and finish the potatoes; cover the finished mash to keep it warm.
- **T−5 minutes:** Measure the finished volume of each recipe and set out enough 250 mL freezer-safe containers for their combined volume.
- **T−0 minutes:** Portion both components into each container in the same volume ratio as the two finished batches. Do not fill above the containers' freezer fill line. Cool the portions promptly, label them with the meal name and date, and freeze.

## Equipment

- One large, heavy-based pot for the stew
- One very large pot for the mash
- One large non-stick frying pan for the onion and spices
- Enough 250 mL freezer-safe containers to hold both full batches
- A measuring jug for calculating and portioning the finished-volume ratio
- Three stovetop elements are useful during the overlap; with two elements, finish the onion before the potatoes need draining.
- A potato masher, colander, kettle, and ladle

## Serving notes

- Each 250 mL container holds both components. Calculate the amount of each as `usable container volume × component volume ÷ combined finished volume`; this uses both full batches without assuming that a serving of stew and a serving of mash occupy the same volume.
- When serving a reheated portion, add torn pane de casa or sourdough with ProActive margarine.
- Accompany the meal with a snifter of port.
